About Rescue 2 Home

Personalized support for people looking to adopt and foster, whatever your circumstances.

All rescue centers should of course offer home checks, rescue backup, and advice, and most do try to do this. However, deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you is not always easy and can be very time-consuming for any rescue to discuss in depth.

Anyone new to adopting, inexperienced in pet ownership, and a little nervous of taking the step to adopt or foster may feel advice is inadequate due to the time constraints put on extremely hard-working rescue staff.

As an experienced pet owner and adopter of both animals and humans, I would like to help bridge that gap. I offer my experience, advice, and time to help you build confidence with your decision to adopt, supporting you in the early days by dropping in if you have an appointment and need to leave your newly adopted pet, or simply having a chat about any concerns you may have.

Why Work With Me?

  • Personalized Matching: Helping you research and find the type of dog more suitable to your specific needs by collaborating with rescues.
  • Safety First: I wear a body camera during client visits to ensure total security and accountability for both myself and the client.
  • Extensive Experience: Owner of four rescue dogs (including an insulin-dependent diabetic) alongside cats and other animals, seamlessly integrated at home.
  • Industry Network: Co-founder of Somerset Animal Information Services alongside my son who works in rescue, connecting you with reputable dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers, and force-free trainers.
Animal welfare is my lifelong passion
