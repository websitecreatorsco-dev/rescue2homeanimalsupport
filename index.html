WELCOME TO RESCUE 2 HOME

Sometimes a little extra support makes all the difference.

Choosing whether to adopt or foster an animal is a big decision. Rescue centres work incredibly hard to support animals and their adopters, but their time is often limited.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support was created to help bridge that gap by providing additional time, experience, advice and reassurance to people who may be new to adoption, inexperienced in pet ownership or simply nervous about taking that step.

ABOUT ME

Supporting people. Supporting animals.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is a new service offering support for people who would like to adopt or foster, whatever their circumstances.

Deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you isn't always easy. For someone new to adopting, inexperienced in pet ownership or a little nervous about taking the step, it can sometimes feel as though there isn't enough time to discuss every question in depth.

As an experienced pet owner and adopter, I want to help bridge that gap by offering my experience, advice and time to help you build confidence in your decision.

I can also work with rescue organisations and research with you to help find the type of dog that may be more suitable for your specific needs.
