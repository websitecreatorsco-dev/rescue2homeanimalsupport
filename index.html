WELCOME

Support for people and their pets.

Bringing a pet into your life can be exciting, but sometimes you need someone to talk things through with.

Whether you are thinking about adopting or fostering, have recently welcomed a new pet, or simply need someone to pop in and give your pet some company, we offer friendly and practical support tailored around you.

We are here to complement the work of rescue centres and other animal professionals — not replace them.

ABOUT ME

A little support can make a big difference.

This is a new service that offers support for people who would like to adopt and foster, whatever their circumstances.

All rescue centres should of course offer home checks, rescue back up and advice and most do try to do this. However, deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you is not always easy and can be very time consuming for any rescue to discuss in depth.

Anyone new to adopting, inexperienced in pet ownership or a little nervous about taking the step to adopt or foster may feel that they need more time, advice or reassurance.

As an experienced pet owner and adopter of both animals and humans, I would like to help bridge that gap by offering my experience, advice and time to help you build confidence with your decision to adopt.

I can also support you in the early days by dropping in if you have an appointment and need to leave your newly adopted pet, or simply by being there to have a chat about any concerns you may have.

I can also try to help you find the type of dog that may be more suitable for your specific circumstances by working with rescue and researching options with you.

EXPERIENCE

Practical experience, with the right people around us.

Animal welfare is my passion. We all need a little help at times to make sure we care for our pets' needs and this is something I will always try to help with.

I am not a trainer, but I do have some knowledge of basic training and dog communication. Where more specialist help is needed, I have access to a network of reputable force-free trainers and behaviourists who can provide appropriate professional support.

I have many pets of my own, including four rescue dogs with very different temperaments, as well as cats and other animals. One of my dogs is also insulin-dependent diabetic.

This has given me plenty of first-hand experience of different animals, personalities, routines and the challenges that can come with bringing animals together in one home.

Working alongside others

We work alongside rescues and work closely with Somerset Animal Information Services .

This provides access to a wider network of people within the pet industry, including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers and other animal professionals.

By working together, we can help people find the right support while keeping the welfare of the animal at the centre of everything we do.
