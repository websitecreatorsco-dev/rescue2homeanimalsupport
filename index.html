ABOUT ME

Helping make the journey a little easier.

This is a service offering support for people who would like to adopt or foster, whatever their circumstances.

Rescue centres should of course offer home checks, rescue back-up and advice, and most do their very best to provide this. However, deciding whether adoption or fostering is right for you isn't always easy.

Rescue staff are extremely hard-working and often have many animals and families to support. Sometimes people who are new to pet ownership or nervous about taking the step can benefit from having additional time to talk things through.

As an experienced pet owner and adopter, I want to help bridge that gap by offering additional experience, advice and time.

I can help you build confidence around your decision, talk through concerns and provide practical support during those important early days.

EXPERIENCE

A genuine passion for animal welfare.

I have many pets of my own, including four rescue dogs with very different temperaments, as well as cats and other animals.

I also work at a local kennels, giving me hands-on experience within an animal care environment.

Alongside this, I run Somerset Animal Information Services , a service connecting pet owners with animal care businesses and services across Somerset.

This gives me a strong network of people working within the pet industry, including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers, trainers and other animal professionals.

My aim is to work alongside rescue centres and other professionals where appropriate, while making it clear that Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is an independent support service — not a rescue centre.
