ABOUT RESCUE 2 HOME

Real experience, practical support.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support was created to give people a little more time, reassurance and practical help when they need it.

Rescue centres work incredibly hard and have their own processes, checks and support in place. However, making the decision to adopt or foster can still feel overwhelming, especially if you are new to pet ownership.

I can help you think through your lifestyle, your home and what type of pet may be suitable for you. I can also help you prepare for those first days together and provide practical support once your pet is home.

Animal welfare is at the heart of everything I do. The aim is simple: to help people feel more confident while making sure the needs of the animal always come first.

EXPERIENCE

I understand that every pet is different.

I have four rescue dogs of my own, all with very different personalities and needs. One is also insulin-dependent diabetic.

I've experienced the realities of bringing animals together in a home, including dogs, cats and other animals.

I am not a professional dog trainer. I do, however, have knowledge of basic training and dog communication and have connections with reputable force-free trainers and behaviourists if specialist help is needed.

The aim is never to pretend I know everything. It's about knowing when someone needs a little extra help and helping them find it.

🐶 Rescue Dogs Experience with dogs with different personalities and needs.
🐱 Multi-Pet Home Experience with dogs, cats and other animals together.
🤝 Local Network Connections with people working across the local pet industry.
🐾 Kennel Experience I also work at a local kennels, giving me hands-on animal care experience.
LOCAL ANIMAL NETWORK

Working alongside the wider animal community.

I run Somerset Animal Information Services and also work at a local kennels.

Through this work I have built connections with people and businesses across the pet industry, including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers and other animal professionals.

Where appropriate, I can point you towards other trusted services when something falls outside what Rescue 2 Home provides.
