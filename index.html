A support service — not a rescue centre

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is not a rescue centre and does not rehome animals. We provide additional support, practical help and reassurance for people and their pets, including people considering adoption or fostering.

A LITTLE EXTRA SUPPORT

Sometimes having someone there makes all the difference.

Bringing a new pet into your life is exciting, but it can also come with plenty of questions. Whether you're thinking about adopting, fostering, settling in a new companion or simply need someone to pop in while you're out, we're here to offer practical help and reassurance.

The aim is simple: to help people feel more confident while making sure the animal in their care is comfortable, safe and getting what they need.

ABOUT RESCUE 2 HOME

Supporting people, pets and the journey in between.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support was created to give people a little more time, advice and practical support when they need it.

Rescue centres work incredibly hard and, quite rightly, carry out their own checks and provide their own rescue support. However, deciding whether adoption or fostering is right for you can sometimes feel like a big step.

We can help you think things through, look at your lifestyle and circumstances, support you with applications and help you prepare for life with a new companion.

We can also provide practical support once your pet is home, including settling-in help and short-term companion sitting.

EXPERIENCE & LOCAL CONNECTIONS

A real connection to the local animal community.

I run Somerset Animal Information Services and I also work at a local kennels, giving me hands-on experience around animals and the people who care for them.

Through this work I have built up a network of people within the local pet industry, including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers, trainers and other animal professionals.

I am also involved in rescue support and animal welfare, which means I understand that every animal and every household is different.

Where something falls outside what I offer, I will always try to point you towards the right person or professional rather than pretending I can do everything.

WHEN YOU NEED MORE HELP

You don't have to figure everything out alone.

We are not trainers or behaviourists. We do have knowledge of basic training and dog communication, and more importantly, we know when it is time to bring in someone with specialist experience.

Through our local network we can help point you towards reputable, force-free trainers and qualified behaviour professionals when appropriate.
