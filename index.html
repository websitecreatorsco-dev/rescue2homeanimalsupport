WELCOME

A little extra support can make a big difference.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is here to provide practical, friendly help to people and their pets.

We are not a rescue centre. We work alongside rescues, adopters, fosterers and pet owners, providing additional support where it can help.

ABOUT RESCUE 2 HOME

Supporting people. Supporting animals.

This is a service offering support to people who would like to adopt or foster, whatever their circumstances.

Choosing whether adoption or fostering is right for you is not always straightforward. Rescue staff work incredibly hard, and sometimes people simply need a little more time to talk things through.

Rescue 2 Home aims to bridge that gap by offering experience, practical advice and reassurance before and during those important early days.

We can also provide practical in-home support for pets, including settling-in help and Comfort & Care drop-ins.

EXPERIENCE & LOCAL CONNECTIONS

Local experience behind the support.

I am the son behind Somerset Animal Information Services and I also work at a local kennels, giving me hands-on experience around animals and the day-to-day realities of animal care.

Through my work with Somerset Animal Information Services, I have built connections with people across the local pet industry, including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers, trainers and other animal professionals.

I use those connections to help point owners towards suitable local support when something falls outside what Rescue 2 Home provides.

✓

We are not a rescue centre, veterinary practice or dog training service. We provide additional practical support and can help connect you with appropriate professionals when needed.
