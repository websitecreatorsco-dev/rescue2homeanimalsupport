ABOUT ME

A little extra support can make a big difference.

This is a new service that offers support for people who would like to adopt and foster, whatever their circumstances.

All rescue centres should of course offer home checks, rescue back-up and advice, and most do try to do this. However, deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you is not always easy or something a rescue can always discuss in depth due to the demands placed on their extremely hard-working staff.

As an experienced pet owner and adopter, I want to help bridge that gap by offering my time, experience and practical advice. The aim is to help you feel more confident about your decision and supported during those important early days.

I can also help you look at what type of dog or companion may be more suitable for your circumstances, working alongside rescues and researching options with you.

Animal welfare is my passion. We all need a little help at times to make sure we are meeting our pets' needs, and that is something I will always try to help with.

EXPERIENCE & NETWORK

Support doesn't stop at the front door.

I am not a trainer, but I do have knowledge of basic training and dog communication. Where more specialist help is needed, I have a network of reputable, force-free trainers and behaviourists that I can point you towards.

I have many pets of my own, including four rescue dogs with very different temperaments, as well as cats and other animals. This has given me plenty of experience of helping different animals live together and managing different needs.

We work alongside rescues and work closely with Somerset Animal Information Services . This provides access to a wider network of people within the pet industry, including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers and other animal professionals.
