A LITTLE EXTRA SUPPORT

You don't have to figure it all out alone.

Bringing a rescue pet into your home can be incredibly rewarding, but it can also feel daunting.

Whether you're thinking about adopting or fostering, have just brought a pet home, or simply need someone to pop in and give them some company, I'm here to help.

ABOUT ME

A helping hand when you need one.

This is a new service that offers support for people who would like to adopt and foster, whatever their circumstances.

All rescue centres should of course offer home checks, rescue back-up and advice, and most do try to do this. However, deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you isn't always easy and can be very time-consuming for already extremely hard-working rescue staff.

If you're new to adopting, inexperienced in pet ownership or simply a little nervous about taking the step, you may feel you need some extra time and reassurance.

That's where I come in. As an experienced pet owner and adopter, I want to help bridge that gap by offering my time, experience and practical support to help you feel more confident about your decision.

I can support you in the early days by dropping in to help if you have an appointment and need to leave your newly adopted pet, or simply by spending some time with you to talk through any concerns you may have.

I can also help you look at what type of dog might be more suitable for your circumstances, working alongside rescue organisations and researching options with you.

EXPERIENCE

Real-life experience with rescue pets.

Animal welfare is my passion. We all need a little help at times to make sure we care for our pets' needs, and this is something I will always try to help with.

I have many pets of my own, including four rescue dogs with very different temperaments. One of them is also insulin-dependent diabetic.

I have integrated them with each other and with my cats and other animals in my home, giving me experience of managing different animals and personalities together.

I am not a trainer, but I do have some knowledge of basic training and dog communication. Where specialist help is needed, I have a network of reputable, force-free trainers and behaviourists that I can point you towards.

WORKING TOGETHER

Working alongside rescues and local animal professionals.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support works alongside rescue organisations and works closely with Somerset Animal Information Services .

Through these connections, I have access to a wider network of people within the pet industry, including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers, trainers and other animal professionals.

The aim is simple: to work together wherever possible to support animals, their welfare and the people who care for them.

🐾 Not a rescue centre

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support does not rehome animals or operate as a rescue centre. We provide additional support to people and their pets and work alongside rescue organisations.
