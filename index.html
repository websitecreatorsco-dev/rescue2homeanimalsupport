We are not a rescue centre.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is an independent support service. We help people who are considering adoption or fostering and provide practical support once a pet comes home. We work alongside rescue organisations and other animal professionals rather than replacing them.

WELCOME

Sometimes having someone to talk to makes all the difference.

Taking on a rescue pet can be incredibly rewarding, but it can also feel like a big step. You might be wondering whether you're ready, which type of pet would suit your home, or what those first few days might actually be like.

Rescue 2 Home is here to provide a bit of extra time, reassurance and practical support when you need it.

ABOUT RESCUE 2 HOME

Practical support, without the pressure.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is a new service offering support to people who would like to adopt or foster, whatever their circumstances.

Rescue centres work incredibly hard and many already provide home checks, advice and rescue back-up. However, they are often extremely busy, and there isn't always the time to talk through every question or concern in as much detail as someone might need.

That's where I hope to help.

Whether you're thinking about adoption for the first time, you're nervous about bringing a new pet home, or you simply want someone to talk to, I can offer time, experience and practical support.

I can also provide support once your pet is home, including settling-in help and short-term companion sitting.

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Built around real experience with animals.

I am the son behind Somerset Animal Information Services and I also work at a local kennels, giving me hands-on experience around animals and the day-to-day realities of animal care.

Animal welfare has always been something that matters greatly to me. I also have experience within the rescue and pet industry and have built a network of people who can help when specialist advice is needed.

I am not a dog trainer or behaviourist. Where something falls outside my own knowledge, I would rather point you towards an appropriately qualified professional.

COMFORT & CARE DROP-INS

A little company while you're away.

Our drop-in sessions take place in your pet's own home. We follow your instructions and keep things familiar, whether that means feeding, fresh water, play, enrichment, cuddles or simply keeping them company.

Sessions are normally one hour, with longer visits available if needed.

We don't provide outdoor dog walking, but we're happy to recommend local dog walkers where appropriate.
