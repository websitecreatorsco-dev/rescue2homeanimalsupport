ABOUT RESCUE 2 HOME

A little extra support can make a big difference.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is a new service offering support to people who would like to adopt or foster, whatever their circumstances.

Rescue centres already do an incredible job providing home checks, rescue back-up and advice. However, deciding whether adoption or fostering is right for you can take time, particularly if you're new to pet ownership or feeling a little nervous about taking that step.

That's where we can help. We offer time, practical advice and reassurance to help you feel more confident before and after bringing a new companion home.

Support can range from talking through what type of dog may suit your lifestyle, helping with applications and preparing for adoption, through to practical support once your new pet is home.
