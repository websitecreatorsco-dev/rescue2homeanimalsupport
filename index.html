About Rescue 2 Home

Tailored support for people looking to adopt and foster, whatever your individual circumstances.

All rescue centers should of course offer home checks, rescue backup, and advice, and most do try. However, deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you is not always easy and can be very time-consuming for hard-working rescue staff.

Anyone new to adopting, inexperienced in pet ownership, and a little nervous may feel standard guidance falls short due to tight time constraints. As an experienced pet owner and adopter of both animals and humans, I am here to bridge that gap.

How I Support Your Adoption Journey

I offer my experience, advice, and time to help you build absolute confidence with your decision. I provide early-day drop-ins if you have appointments and need to leave your newly adopted pet, or a safe space to chat through any concerns.

Dog Matching & Breed Research

I help you find the specific type of dog suited to your lifestyle by working closely with rescue centers and conducting collaborative research with you.

Personal Home Environment & Experience

I own four rescue dogs with vastly different temperaments—including one insulin-dependent diabetic—successfully integrated alongside cats and other animals. I am not a trainer, but I understand basic communication and partner with reputable force-free trainers.

Safety Commitment: I wear a body camera during all client visits to ensure total security and accountability for both myself and you.
