WELCOME

Support for people. Support for animals.

Taking on a new pet is exciting, but it can also feel like a big step. Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is here to provide that extra bit of time, reassurance and practical help when you need it.

We work alongside rescues and animal welfare organisations rather than being a rescue ourselves. Our aim is simply to help people feel more confident and prepared while keeping the welfare of the animal at the heart of everything we do.

ABOUT ME

A little extra support can make a big difference.

This is a new service that offers support for people who would like to adopt and foster, whatever their circumstances.

All rescue centres should of course offer home checks, rescue back up and advice and most do try to do this. However, deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you is not always easy and can be very time consuming for any rescue to discuss in depth.

Anyone new to adopting, inexperienced in pet ownership or a little nervous about taking the step may feel that they need more time, advice or reassurance than a busy rescue centre can always provide.

As an experienced pet owner and adopter of animals, I would like to help bridge that gap by offering my experience, advice and time.

I can help you build confidence with your decision to adopt, support you during the early days and provide practical help if you have an appointment and need to leave your newly adopted pet.

I can also help you research what type of dog or companion may be more suitable for your circumstances, working alongside rescue and helping you consider the needs of both you and the animal.

Animal welfare is my passion. We all need a little help sometimes to make sure we are meeting our pets’ needs, and that is something I will always try to help with.

EXPERIENCE

Real-life experience with rescue animals.

I am not a trainer, but I do have some knowledge of basic training and dog communication. I also have a network of reputable force-free trainers who I can direct you towards should you feel specialist help is needed.

I have many pets of my own, including four rescue dogs, each with very different temperaments. One of my dogs is also insulin-dependent diabetic.

I have integrated them with each other and with my cats and other animals in my home, giving me plenty of experience of managing different animals and personalities together.

🐾

We are here to support the journey.

We don't replace rescues, trainers, behaviourists or veterinary professionals. Instead, we provide additional practical support and help connect you with the right people when needed.

WORKING TOGETHER

Working alongside rescues and local animal services.

We work alongside rescues and work closely with Somerset Animal Information Services, giving us access to a wider network of people working within the pet industry.

This includes dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers and other animal-related services.

By working together, we can help people make informed decisions and give dogs, cats and other animals the best possible chance of finding the right home.
