ABOUT RESCUE 2 HOME

Supporting people. Supporting animals.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is a service offering support to people who would like to adopt or foster, whatever their circumstances.

Rescue centres already work incredibly hard to provide home checks, rescue back-up and advice. However, deciding whether adoption or fostering is right for you isn't always easy and there isn't always enough time for every question to be discussed in depth.

For anyone new to adopting, inexperienced in pet ownership or simply nervous about taking that next step, I aim to provide an additional source of experience, advice and reassurance.

I can help you build confidence in your decision, talk through concerns and, where appropriate, help you research the type of animal that may be best suited to your circumstances.

I can also provide practical support during those important early days after bringing an animal home.

EXPERIENCE & SUPPORT

Experience you can talk to.

Animal welfare is my passion. We all need a little help at times to make sure we care for our pets' needs and this is something I will always try to help with.

I have many pets of my own, including four rescue dogs with very different temperaments, as well as cats and other animals.

I am not a dog trainer, but I do have knowledge of basic training and dog communication and have a network of reputable force-free trainers who I can direct you towards if you need additional help.

✓

My role is not to replace rescue centres, trainers, vets or other professionals. Instead, I provide additional support and can help connect you with the right people where appropriate.

ANIMAL WELFARE NETWORK

Connected to the local animal community.

I run Somerset Animal Information Services , a local animal information and support network.

I also work at a local kennels, giving me practical day-to-day experience working with dogs and supporting their welfare and care.

Through Somerset Animal Information Services and my work within the animal care industry, I have built a network of people across the pet industry, including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers, trainers and other animal professionals.

This network allows us to work alongside other professionals and rescue organisations where appropriate, helping people access the right support for themselves and their animals.
