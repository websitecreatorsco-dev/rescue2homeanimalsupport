ABOUT RESCUE 2 HOME

Supporting people. Supporting pets.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is a new independent service offering support to people who would like to adopt or foster, as well as practical care for pets in their own homes.

All rescue centres should, of course, offer home checks, rescue back-up and advice, and many do an excellent job of doing so. However, deciding whether adoption or fostering is right for you can be a big decision.

Rescue staff are incredibly busy, and sometimes people simply need a little more time to talk things through, ask questions and build their confidence.

That's where Rescue 2 Home can help. We can provide additional time, experience, practical advice and reassurance before, during and after the adoption or fostering process.

We can also provide practical support once your new companion is home, including settling-in support and short-term companion sitting.

EXPERIENCE & SUPPORT

Experience you can talk to.

Animal welfare is a passion, and we all need a little help sometimes to make sure we are meeting our pets' needs.

There are four rescue dogs in the family, each with very different temperaments, alongside cats and other animals.

One of the dogs is also insulin-dependent diabetic, providing experience of managing additional care requirements.

We are not trainers, but we have knowledge of basic training and dog communication, alongside a network of reputable force-free trainers and other professionals who can be recommended where appropriate.

✓

Our role is to provide additional support, not replace rescue centres, veterinary professionals, trainers or behaviourists.

ANIMAL WELFARE NETWORK

I am the founder of Somerset Animal Information Services , a local animal information and business directory supporting people across Somerset.

I also work at a local kennels, giving me hands-on experience within an animal care environment.

Through my work and connections, I have built a network of people within the pet industry, including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers, trainers and other animal professionals.

NEW SERVICE

Comfort & Care Drop-Ins

£25 per hour

Sometimes your pets just need some company, love and a little extra attention while you're out. Our custom drop-in sessions provide exactly that, in the comfort of their own home.

Whether your pet is a playful pup, a senior cat or a small furry friend, we tailor the time around your instructions and their individual needs.

Standard sessions are one hour. Longer sessions can be arranged for an additional cost.
