A LITTLE EXTRA SUPPORT

Sometimes having someone there makes all the difference.

Bringing a rescue animal into your home can be exciting, but it can also come with questions, nerves and uncertainty.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is here to offer practical, friendly support around adoption, fostering, settling in and everyday companion care.

ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Beverley.

This is a new service that offers support for people who would like to adopt and foster, whatever their circumstances.

All rescue centres should of course offer home checks, rescue back-up and advice, and most do try to do this. However, deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you is not always easy and can be very time-consuming for any rescue to discuss in depth.

Anyone new to adopting, inexperienced in pet ownership or simply a little nervous about taking the step to adopt or foster may feel that they need more time, advice and reassurance.

As an experienced pet owner and adopter, I want to help bridge that gap by offering my experience, advice and time to help you feel more confident about your decision to adopt.

I can also support you during those early days, including dropping in if you need to leave your newly adopted pet for an appointment, or simply being there to talk through any concerns you may have.

I can also try to help you find the type of dog that may be more suitable for your circumstances by working alongside rescue organisations and researching options with you.

EXPERIENCE & SUPPORT

Practical experience, with a personal approach.

Animal welfare is my passion. We all need a little help at times to make sure we care for our pets' needs, and this is something I will always try to help with.

I am not a trainer, but I do have knowledge of basic training and dog communication. I also have a network of reputable, force-free trainers and behaviourists that I can direct you towards should you need more specialist help.

I have many pets of my own, including four rescue dogs, each with very different temperaments. One of my dogs is also insulin-dependent diabetic.

I have experience integrating dogs, cats and other animals within my home and understanding that every animal is an individual.

WORKING TOGETHER

Working alongside rescues and local animal services.

We work alongside rescue organisations and work closely with Somerset Animal Information Services .

This gives us access to a wider network of people within the pet industry, including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers, trainers, behaviourists and other animal professionals.

The aim is simple: to work together to support animals and their people, helping make sure welfare needs are understood and that owners know where to turn when they need extra help.

2. Services & Bookings

Services are subject to availability and are arranged directly with the client.

We generally require at least 24 hours' notice for bookings. Short-notice requests may be considered where availability allows, but cannot be guaranteed.

A booking is confirmed once the service, date, time and price have been agreed.

3. Payments

Prices are stated clearly when services are arranged. Payment arrangements will be confirmed with the client before the service takes place.

Prices may be changed in the future, but any agreed price for an existing booking will be honoured unless otherwise agreed with the client.

Our adoption and fostering support is intended to provide additional guidance and reassurance.

We do not make adoption or fostering decisions, approve applications or guarantee that a particular animal will be suitable or available.

Final decisions regarding adoption and fostering remain with the relevant rescue organisation.

5. Pet & Companion Support

Clients remain responsible for providing accurate information about their animal, including relevant behavioural, medical, dietary and safety information.

We will follow reasonable instructions provided by the client, but we cannot guarantee the behaviour or actions of an animal.

6. Veterinary & Specialist Advice

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support does not provide veterinary diagnosis, treatment or emergency veterinary care.

Where appropriate, clients may be advised to contact a veterinary professional, qualified trainer, behaviourist or other specialist.

7. Cancellation & Rescheduling

Our standard cancellation notice is 24 hours. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may incur a cancellation charge up to the full value of the service.

Nothing in these terms removes or restricts any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.

We take reasonable care when providing our services. However, animals can behave unpredictably and clients must provide accurate information about known risks.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability where doing so would be unlawful, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence or any other liability that cannot legally be excluded.
