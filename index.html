WELCOME TO RESCUE 2 HOME

Independent support for people and their animals.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is an independent animal support service. We are not a rescue centre or animal rescue organisation.

We provide additional support to people who are considering adoption or fostering, as well as practical support for existing pet owners.

This includes helping people feel more confident about welcoming a rescue animal into their home, supporting them during the settling-in period and providing our Comfort & Care Drop-In service when pets need some extra company and attention at home.

ABOUT RESCUE 2 HOME

Supporting people. Supporting animals.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support is an independent service created to provide additional support to people and their animals.

One of the main areas of support is helping people who would like to adopt or foster. All rescue centres should, of course, offer home checks, rescue back-up and advice, and most do try to do this.

However, deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you is not always easy. Rescue staff are extremely busy, and there isn't always enough time to have the longer conversations that someone new to pet ownership may need.

As an experienced pet owner and adopter of both animals and humans, I would like to help bridge that gap by offering my experience, advice and time to help you build confidence in your decision.

I can also provide support during those important early days, including visiting to help if you have an appointment and need to leave your newly adopted pet, or simply being there to talk through any concerns you may have.

I can also try to help you find the type of dog that may be more suitable for your specific needs by working with rescue and researching with you.

Rescue 2 Home also provides Comfort & Care Drop-Ins for pets in their own homes.

These visits are designed for pets who need some company, attention, feeding, enrichment or reassurance while their owners are away.

COMFORT & CARE DROP-INS

A little extra care while you're away.

Sometimes your pets just need some company, love and a little extra attention while you're out.

Our Comfort & Care Drop-In sessions take place in the comfort of your pet's own home and are tailored completely around your instructions and their normal routine.

Whether you have a playful pup, a senior cat or a small furry friend, we can provide companionship and care while you're away.

✓

Standard sessions are one hour and give us plenty of time to settle your pet, provide their care and make sure they are comfortable.

🥣 Feeding & Fresh Water Keeping your pet on their usual routine.
🧸 Play & Enrichment Favourite toys, games and activities to keep boredom at bay.
❤️ Cuddles & Company Plenty of affection, reassurance and companionship.
🏠 Home Comforts Anything else needed to keep your pet happy and comfortable.

AN IMPORTANT NOTE

Independent support — not a rescue centre.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support does not take animals into rescue, operate kennels or rehome animals.

Adoption and fostering support is designed to provide additional time, experience and reassurance alongside the rescue organisation you are working with.
