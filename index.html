ABOUT ME

Helping people feel confident about giving a pet a home.

This is a new service that offers support for people who would like to adopt or foster, whatever their circumstances.

All rescue centres should of course offer home checks, rescue back-up and advice, and most do try to do this. However, deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you is not always easy and can be very time consuming for rescue staff to discuss in depth.

Anyone new to adopting, inexperienced in pet ownership or a little nervous about taking that step may feel they need more time, advice or reassurance.

I would like to help bridge that gap by offering my experience, advice and time to help you build confidence in your decision to adopt or foster.

I can also support you during the early days by dropping in if you have an appointment and need to leave your newly adopted pet, or simply by being there to talk through any concerns you may have.

I can also try to help you find the type of dog that may be more suitable for your circumstances by working alongside rescue organisations and researching options with you.
