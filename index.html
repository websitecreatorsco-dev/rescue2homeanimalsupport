ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Beverley.

This is a new service that offers support for people who would like to adopt and foster, whatever their circumstances.

All rescue centres should of course offer home checks, rescue back up and advice, and most do try to do this. However, deciding if adoption or fostering is right for you is not always easy and can be very time consuming for rescue teams to discuss in depth.

Anyone new to adopting, inexperienced in pet ownership or a little nervous about taking the step may sometimes feel that they need more time, advice and reassurance.

I would like to help bridge that gap by offering my experience, advice and time to help you build confidence with your decision to adopt or foster.

I can also support you during those early days, including dropping in if you have an appointment and need to leave your newly adopted pet, or simply being there to talk through any concerns.

I can also try to help you find the type of dog that may be more suitable for your circumstances by working alongside rescue and researching options with you.

✓ Welfare First

Animal welfare is my passion. We all need a little help at times to make sure we care for our pets' needs, and this is something I will always try to help with.

I am not a trainer, but I do have some knowledge of basic training and dog communication, as well as a network of reputable force-free trainers to direct you to should you feel you need further help.

I have many pets of my own, including four rescue dogs, each with very different temperaments. I have experience of integrating them with each other, as well as with cats and other animals in my home.

🐾
Working alongside the wider animal community

We work alongside rescues and work closely with Somerset Animal Information Services, helping maintain connections with people across the local pet industry including dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers and other animal professionals.

Together, we can help people feel more confident about welcoming an animal into their lives while making sure the welfare needs of both the animal and their new family are considered.
